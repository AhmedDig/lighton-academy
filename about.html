Our Story

In 2023, Ahmed Hammad – a software engineer who studied in the United States and returned to the West Bank in 2018 – saw a glaring gap: talented, hardworking individuals lacked the practical skills needed for high‑level employment. Determined to change that, he founded Lighton Academy. What started as a small training initiative has grown into a beacon of hope for hundreds of students, proving that with the right skills, anyone can rise.

We don’t just teach theory. We build real‑world capabilities in English, marketing, content creation, and technology. Our students don’t just learn – they earn.
